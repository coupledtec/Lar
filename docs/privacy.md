# Lar privacy policy

*Last updated: October 26, 2026*

**Lar collects no data.** It has no server, no account of its own, no analytics, ads or tracking.

## What Lar keeps, and where

- **The keys of the accounts you connect** (LG ThinQ, Smart Life/Tuya, SmartThings, Amazon/Alexa, Microsoft/Xbox,
  the intercom's server): in your device's Keychain. On the iPhone they also go to your Apple Watch, so it works by itself.
- **Scenes, names, favorites, rooms and preferences:** on the device and, if you pair them, synced between your Mac
  and your iPhone over your home network, encrypted. In the App Store versions, also through your iCloud (Apple's
  CloudKit), so they're the same on all your devices and in a shared home. The accounts' keys never go to iCloud.
- **Your home's location**, if you mark one, for the "when arriving" and "when leaving" scenes: only on the iPhone.
- **The air conditioner's temperature and usage history:** only on the device.

## Who Lar talks to

Only the services you connect yourself, with your own keys, to show and control your devices: LG, Tuya, Samsung
SmartThings, Amazon (Alexa), Microsoft (Xbox), your intercom's SIP server and the devices on your network (TVs). Each has its own
privacy policy. Lar also asks GitHub whether there's a new version (only the Mac app downloaded from GitHub).

## Microphone and location

- **Microphone:** only during a call with the intercom.
- **Location:** only for the arriving/leaving scenes and, if you turn it on, for the iPhone to reopen Lar by itself.
  Nothing is sent to anyone.

## Deleting everything

Remove the accounts in Lar's Settings and delete the app. No data is kept outside your devices.
